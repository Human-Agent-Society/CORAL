# Preregistered external-validation confirmation ladder

## Motivation

The existing natural-agent and Circle Packing matrices use eight paired
blocks. They are useful discovery and operational-validity studies, but their
failure is not generally evidence of absence. An outcome-free normal
approximation shows that the natural `multi-partition` gate has only about
20.4% power at a true `+0.20` random-SD effect when paired-block SD is `0.5`.
The Circle discovery gate has about 23.8% power at a true `+0.02` score effect
when paired-block SD is `0.04`.

No natural-agent or Circle Packing formal result directory existed when this
extension was frozen. The discovery matrices remain unchanged, but a positive
article-level claim now requires fresh confirmation.

## Common decision structure

Both external confirmations retain permanent partition as the direct
migration control. A pass requires:

1. the registered practical point-effect floor versus global;
2. the registered practical point-effect floor versus partition;
3. a positive one-sided 95% paired lower bound for both contrasts;
4. complete quotas, isolation, and integrity audits; and
5. a realized migration followed by a real migrant submission in every
   multi-island block.

The positive claim is the conjunction of both effect claims. Each component
uses one-sided alpha 0.05 as an intersection-union test. Discovery data select
or motivate a cell but never enter the fresh confirmation estimates.

## Natural-agent confirmation

The eight-block natural matrix remains a discovery/behavioral audit at the
frozen `N=512, K=64, B=16,384` anchor and includes the hard Smooth negative
control. If the external-validation ladder is launched, it is followed
regardless of discovery direction by:

- 40 fresh Rugged landscape blocks from a new private seed namespace;
- zero landscape-seed overlap with v4, v5, v6, or extreme phases;
- the same eight agents, model, prompt, budget, quotas, three topologies, and
  migration schedule as the natural discovery arm;
- a Latin-square condition order; and
- no Smooth confirmation expansion, because the confirmatory causal claim is
  the Rugged migration effect and Smooth already serves as a negative control.

Practical floors remain `multi-global >= +0.25` and
`multi-partition >= +0.10` held-out random SD. Forty blocks give at least
80% approximate component power when the true effects are `+0.35/+0.20` and
paired-effect SD is at most `0.5`.

The new private landscape bundle, runner, auditor, and analyzer must be
source-hash registered while all natural result roots are absent. This
protocol does not authorize launching an unbound implementation.

## Circle Packing confirmation

The unchanged discovery matrix covers budgets 32, 64, and 128 with eight
paired stochastic blocks. After all 72 discovery cells pass integrity audit,
one budget is selected deterministically:

1. among budgets whose point effects versus global and partition are both at
   least `+0.01` and whose migration-compliance gate passes, choose the lowest;
2. if none qualifies, maximize the smaller point-effect surplus over `+0.01`;
3. use the lower budget as the deterministic tie-breaker.

The selected budget is then tested on 32 fresh model-service blocks with the
same four agents, grader, sandbox, quotas, topologies, and fixed migration
cadence. The discovery blocks are not pooled into confirmation. Thirty-two
blocks give about 88.2% component power at a true `+0.02` score effect when
paired-effect SD is `0.04`.

The selector, fresh result root, runner, audit, and analyzer must be
source-hash registered before any discovery outcome is read.

## Execution and claim boundary

External validation begins only after the scripted extreme confirmation
passes. Natural and Circle confirmations answer different external-validity
questions and cannot rescue one another. A pass supports the registered task
and agent population only; a failure is evidence of absence only within the
paired-dispersion range for which the corresponding confirmation was powered.
